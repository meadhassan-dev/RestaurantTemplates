Our Story

About Le Petit Bistro

A Parisian journey in every dish.

A Legacy of French Cuisine

Le Petit Bistro was founded in 1998 by Chef Pierre Moreau, who brought his family's century-old recipes from Lyon to New York. What began as a small 30-seat bistro has grown into one of the city's most beloved French restaurants.

Pierre's philosophy is simple: honor the traditions of French cooking while embracing the finest seasonal ingredients. Every sauce is made from scratch, every stock is simmered for hours, and every plate is crafted with the precision and passion that defines great French cuisine.

Over the past 26 years, we've served over 50,000 guests, celebrated countless milestones, and remained committed to providing an authentic Parisian dining experience right here in New York.

Our Timeline

Milestones from our journey.

1998
Grand Opening

Chef Pierre Moreau opens Le Petit Bistro with 30 seats in the heart of New York.

2003
First Accolade

Awarded "Best French Bistro" by New York Magazine.

2010
Bistro Expansion

Renovated and expanded to 70 seats, adding a private dining room.

2018
20th Anniversary

Celebrated two decades of French culinary excellence with a special prix-fixe menu.

2023
New Chapter

Chef Camille Dubois joins as Pastry Chef, earning acclaim for her dessert program.
