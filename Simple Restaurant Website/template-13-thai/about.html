Our Story

Bringing the spirit of Bangkok to LA.

Spicy Beginnings

Bangkok Bites was founded in 2015 by Chef Somchai Rattana, who left his family's restaurant in Bangkok to share the authentic street food of his homeland with Los Angeles. What started as a small food truck has grown into a beloved Thai eatery.

Every dish at Bangkok Bites tells a story of tradition, family, and the vibrant street food culture of Thailand. Our chefs are trained in Bangkok, learning the art of balancing sweet, sour, salty, and spicy — the four pillars of Thai cuisine.

We import key ingredients directly from Thailand, source the freshest local produce, and refuse to compromise on authenticity. No shortcuts, no fusion gimmicks — just real Thai food made with love.

Our Journey

From food truck to local favorite.

2015
Food Truck Launch

Somchai launches Bangkok Bites food truck, serving authentic Pad Thai and Green Curry.

2017
First Restaurant

We open our first brick-and-mortar location on Siam Avenue in Los Angeles.

2020
Community Support

Launched meal donation program, serving 10,000+ meals to frontline workers and families in need.

2023
Expansion

Expanded our kitchen and added a full bar with Thai-inspired cocktails.

2025
Award Winner
