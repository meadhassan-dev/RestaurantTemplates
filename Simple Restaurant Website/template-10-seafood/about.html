Our Story

A passion for the sea since 2002

Ocean to Table

The Blue Catch was founded by Captain Jake Marlowe, a third-generation fisherman who wanted to bring the taste of the sea to the heart of the city. We source our seafood directly from sustainable fisheries and serve it within 48 hours of being caught.

Our kitchen is led by Chef Maria Santos, who combines classic coastal techniques with bold global flavors. Every dish celebrates the natural beauty of the ocean's bounty, from the cold waters of Maine to the warm currents of the Gulf.

Our Journey

Milestones from the deep

2002
Setting Sail

Captain Jake Marlowe opens The Blue Catch as a small oyster bar near the Seaport District with 20 seats.

2010
Sustainable Pledge

Became one of the first NYC restaurants to achieve Marine Stewardship Council certification.

2018
Harbor View Location

Relocated to our current flagship location on Harbor View with panoramic waterfront dining.

2023
James Beard Finalist

Chef Maria Santos named a James Beard Award finalist for Best Chef: Northeast.

2026
24 Years of Freshness

Celebrating nearly a quarter century of bringing the ocean's finest to New York's tables.

Our Promise

Committed to ocean stewardship

Sustainability

We partner only with fisheries that practice responsible harvesting and protect marine ecosystems.

Freshness

Seafood is never frozen on the boat and arrives at our kitchen within 48 hours of being caught.

Traceability

Every fish on our menu can be traced back to the boat and the waters it was caught in.

Excellence

We hold ourselves to the highest standards of quality, service, and culinary craftsmanship.
