Our Story

A legacy of culinary excellence

The Beginning

Maison Élégance was founded in 1998 by Chef Laurent Dubois, a visionary who believed that dining should be a transcendent experience. Trained at Le Cordon Bleu and having honed his craft in Michelin-starred kitchens across Europe, Chef Dubois brought a philosophy of perfection to every plate.

What began as a 30-seat gem in Manhattan has become one of America's most celebrated fine dining establishments, earning two Michelin stars and a reputation for uncompromising excellence.

Our Philosophy

We source the rarest ingredients from around the globe — Japanese Wagyu, French truffles, Italian olive oil — and transform them into edible art. Every course is paired with wines from our 800-bottle cellar, curated by our master sommelier.

Our kitchen operates with the precision of a symphony, where every element on the plate serves a purpose. We believe in seasonality, sustainability, and the pursuit of perfection in every detail.

Excellence is not a skill. It is an attitude.
