Our Story

El Fiesta Taqueria was founded in 2010 by Carlos Hernandez, who learned the art of Mexican cooking from his grandmother in Oaxaca. Every recipe on our menu is a family heirloom, passed down through generations and made with love.

From our handmade tortillas to our slow-cooked carnitas, we use traditional techniques and the freshest ingredients. Our salsa bar features five housemade salsas ranging from mild to inferno.

¡Bienvenidos, amigos!

Our Promise

We source the finest ingredients — organic produce from local farms, authentic Mexican cheeses, and premium cuts of meat. Our masa is made from non-GMO corn, nixtamalized in-house for authentic flavor and texture.

We believe great Mexican food starts with great ingredients and a whole lot of heart. Every dish is made to order, just like abuela used to make.
