Our Story

From a farmers' market stall to a beloved community kitchen.

How It All Began

Vegan Bowl was born in 2018 from a simple belief: that plant-based food can be exciting, nourishing, and kind to the planet. Founder Maya Green started with a small farmers' market stall in Portland, offering just three bowl recipes made from locally sourced organic ingredients.

The response was overwhelming. Customers lined up every weekend for her vibrant, nutrient-packed creations. Within a year, Maya opened the first Vegan Bowl kitchen, transforming her passion into a thriving community hub.

Today, we source from over 15 local farms, serve hundreds of guests daily, and remain committed to our core mission: making plant-based eating accessible, exciting, and delicious for everyone.
