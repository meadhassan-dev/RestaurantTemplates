Our Heritage

Bringing India's culinary soul to your table since 1990

A Tradition of Taste

Punjab Palace was born from a dream to share the authentic flavors of India with New York City. Founded by the Kohli family, our recipes have been passed down through four generations of master chefs.

Every spice is carefully sourced from the finest regions of India — saffron from Kashmir, cardamom from Kerala, and chilies from Andhra Pradesh. Our kitchen is a symphony of aromas, colors, and textures that tell the story of India's diverse culinary landscape.

Fresh, organic ingredients sourced daily
Traditional tandoor clay oven cooking
Hand-ground spice blends

Our Journey

Milestones in our story

1990
The Beginning

The Kohli family opens Punjab Palace in a small storefront in Little India with just six tables.

2000
A New Home

Relocated to our current landmark location on Curry Lane, expanding to 120 seats.

2010
National Recognition

Featured in Food & Wine magazine as one of America's best Indian restaurants.

2020
Michelin Star

Awarded our first Michelin star, a testament to our commitment to excellence.

2026
Continuing the Legacy

Celebrating 36 years of authentic Indian cuisine with a new generation of Kohli chefs.
