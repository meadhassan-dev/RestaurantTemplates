Our Story

Low & slow since 2001.

How It All Began

Smokehouse BBQ started in a backyard pit in Nashville in 2001. Founder Big Jim Walker began hosting weekend barbecues for friends and family, experimenting with different woods, rubs, and smoking techniques until he perfected his craft.

The demand grew so much that Jim quit his job and opened Smokehouse BBQ in a small roadside shack. 25 years later, we've become a Nashville institution, known far and wide for our authentic, wood-fired barbecue.

We use only hickory and oak wood, smoking our meats low and slow for up to 16 hours. Every rack of ribs, every brisket, every link of sausage is a testament to the art of Southern BBQ. No shortcuts. Just real fire, real wood, real flavor.
