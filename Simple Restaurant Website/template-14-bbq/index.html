Our Story

Smokehouse BBQ started in a backyard pit in 2001. What began as weekend gatherings with friends and family grew into a beloved Southern smokehouse known for authentic, wood-fired barbecue.

We use only hickory and oak wood, smoking our meats low and slow for up to 16 hours.
