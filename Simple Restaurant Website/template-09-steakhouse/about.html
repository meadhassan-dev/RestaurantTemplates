The Story

Three decades of uncompromising quality

Fire & Tradition

The Black Iron was founded in 1995 by master butcher James Croft, who believed that a great steak starts with the finest beef. We dry-age our prime cuts for a minimum of 28 days in our Himalayan salt room, ensuring unmatched flavor and tenderness.

Our 1800°F broiler gives each steak the perfect char while locking in the juices. From the pasture to your plate, every step is handled with reverence for the craft. We source only USDA Prime and authentic Japanese Wagyu, hand-selected by our master butcher.
