Our Story

Honoring tradition, embracing innovation

Our Journey

Sakura Sushi Bar was founded in 2005 by Chef Hiroshi Tanaka, a Tokyo-born master who trained for 15 years in some of Ginza's most prestigious sushi-ya. His vision was to bring authentic Edomae sushi to New York, using only the freshest seafood sourced directly from Tsukiji Market.

What started as a small 12-seat counter has grown into one of Manhattan's most celebrated sushi destinations, while never losing the intimate, respectful atmosphere that defines true Japanese dining.

Shokunin no tamashii — The soul of the craftsman lives in every piece we serve.

Our Philosophy

At Sakura, every grain of rice matters. Our sushi rice is seasoned with aged red vinegar, balanced precisely to complement the fish. Each slice is cut at a specific angle to enhance texture and flavor release.

We believe in sustainability as much as quality. Our seafood is responsibly sourced, and we work directly with fishermen who share our commitment to ocean stewardship.
