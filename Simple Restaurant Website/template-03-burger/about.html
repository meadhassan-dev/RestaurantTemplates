How It All Began

Burger Republic was born in 2012 in a tiny food truck parked outside a Los Angeles music venue. Founder Jake Morrison, a classically trained chef turned burger obsessive, set out to prove that a burger could be both craveable and crafted.

Word spread fast. Soon there were lines around the block. Within a year, the food truck became a brick-and-mortar location, and the rest is delicious history.

Our Promise

We source our beef from family-run farms in the Midwest where cattle are grass-fed and humanely raised. Our buns are baked fresh every morning by a local artisan bakery. Even our pickles are house-made.

We believe a great burger is more than food — it's an experience. Every ingredient is chosen with care, every patty is hand-formed, and every order is cooked to order. No freezers, no shortcuts, no compromises.
