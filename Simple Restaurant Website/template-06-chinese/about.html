Our Story

A legacy of authentic flavors since 1985

Our Heritage

Welcome to Golden Dragon, where centuries-old recipes meet contemporary elegance. Our chefs bring traditions from the imperial kitchens of Beijing to your table, using only the finest ingredients sourced directly from China.

Every dish tells a story — from the hand-pulled noodles of Lanzhou to the delicate dim sum of Hong Kong. We invite you to experience the rich tapestry of Chinese culinary heritage in an atmosphere that blends modern sophistication with ancient traditions.

Founded by the Wang family in 1985, Golden Dragon started as a small noodle shop in the heart of Chinatown. Through dedication, passion, and an unwavering commitment to authenticity, we have grown into one of New York's most celebrated Chinese restaurants.

Our Journey

Milestones that shaped Golden Dragon

1985
Humble Beginnings

The Wang family opens a small noodle shop in Chinatown with just 8 tables and a dream.

1995
First Expansion

Moved to our current location on Dragon Street, expanding to 80 seats and hiring our first wok master from Sichuan.

2005
New York's Finest

Awarded "Best Chinese Restaurant" by New York Magazine. Our Peking Duck becomes a city-wide sensation.

2015
Michelin Recognition

Received our first Michelin Bib Gourmand, recognizing our commitment to quality and value.

2025
40 Years of Excellence

Celebrating four decades of bringing authentic Chinese cuisine to New York with a renovated space and expanded menu.
