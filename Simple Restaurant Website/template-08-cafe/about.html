Our Story

A cozy corner born from a love for great coffee

A Labor of Love

Mocha & Mint began as a dream between two friends who believed that great coffee could bring people together. What started as a tiny cart in the park has blossomed into your favorite neighborhood café.

We source our beans from sustainable farms around the world and roast them in small batches to ensure every cup is perfect. Our pastries are baked fresh daily using organic, locally-sourced ingredients. Every corner of our café is designed to feel like an extension of your living room — warm, inviting, and full of character.

Sustainably sourced coffee beans
Freshly baked pastries every morning
Community-driven & LGBTQ+ friendly

Our Journey

From a cart to your neighborhood favorite

2015
The Cart

Maya and Jen start a coffee cart in Prospect Park, serving pour-overs with beans they roasted in their apartment.

2017
First Location

Open our first brick-and-mortar café on Brew Street with just 12 seats and a shared pastry partnership.

2020
Community Anchor

During challenging times, we pivoted to community-supported coffee deliveries and never stopped serving.

2023
Brooklyn's Best

Named "Best Coffee Shop in Brooklyn" by The Village Voice. Expanded our kitchen for full pastry production.

2026
Growing Our Family

Celebrating 11 years with a second location coming soon and our first-ever coffee subscription box.
