Our Story

From Naples to New York — a pizza journey.

Born in Naples, Raised in NYC

Dough & Fire was founded in 2015 by Marco Rossi, a third-generation pizzaiolo who trained in Naples under the watchful eye of his nonno. After years perfecting his craft in Italy, Marco brought his family's pizza traditions to New York City.

Our wood-fired oven, imported from Naples, burns at 900°F to create that perfect leopard-spotted crust in just 90 seconds. We use only San Marzano tomatoes grown on the slopes of Mount Vesuvius, fresh mozzarella di bufala from Campania, and 00 flour milled in Italy.

Every pizza is a labor of love — from the hand-kneaded dough that ferments for 72 hours to the carefully sourced toppings that honor Italian tradition while embracing local seasonality.

Our Philosophy

Simple ingredients, extraordinary results.

72-Hour Dough

Our dough is cold-fermented for 72 hours, developing complex flavor and that signature light, airy texture.

900°F Perfection

Our wood-fired oven reaches 900°F, cooking each pizza in 90 seconds for the perfect char and chew.

Handmade Daily

Every pizza is stretched by hand, topped with care, and baked to order. No shortcuts, no compromises.
