The Art of AURELIA

Est. 2014

A Legacy Written in Fire & Finesse

What began as a twelve-seat kitchen hidden in a brownstone has blossomed into one of the city's most coveted dining rooms. AURELIA was founded on a singular conviction: that a meal should be an experience for all five senses.

Our dining room is a study in contrasts — candlelight against dark stone, crisp linen against aged brass, whispers against the crackle of the open fire. It is an atmosphere composed with the same care as our tasting menu.

Our Philosophy

Three Pillars of Excellence

01

Ingredient Purity

We chase the extraordinary in the everyday — a single tomato, an aged vinegar, a rare mushroom. Quality begins at the source, and we spare no journey to reach it.

02

Precision Craft

From the hand-hammered copper pans to the ember temperatures recorded to the degree, every detail in our kitchen is an act of devotion.

03

Gracious Service

Our maître d', sommeliers and captains anticipate before you ask. Hospitality here is intuition, whispered and never imposed.
