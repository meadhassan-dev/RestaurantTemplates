Est. 2018

A legacy written in soil & ember

twelve seats, one conviction

What began as a twelve-seat garden counter tucked behind a community greenhouse has blossomed into one of the city's most beloved dining rooms. VERDANTE was founded on a singular conviction: that vegetables deserve the same reverence as the finest cut of meat.

Our room is a study in living contrasts — candlelight against living walls, smoke against citrus, crisp linen against reclaimed oak. It is an atmosphere grown with the same patience as our produce.

Our Philosophy

Three pillars of the garden

soil · flame · welcome
01

Vegetable First

We build plates from the soil up. A single heirloom tomato, a wild foraged mushroom, a just-picked herb — grown, not imported. The fire only deepens what the earth already made perfect.

02

Fire-Led Craft

Oak, almond and grape vine feed our open hearth. Every temperature is recorded to the degree, every ember turned by hand — an ancient technique treated with modern precision.

03

Gracious Hospitality

Our maître d', gardeners and sommeliers anticipate before you ask. Hospitality here is intuition — warm, unhurried, and never imposed.
