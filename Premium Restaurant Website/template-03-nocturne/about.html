Est. 2021 a love letter to the night
Est. 2021

A legacy written in candlelight

What began as a fourteen-seat chef's counter tucked behind a jazz club has become one of Chicago's most beloved rooms. NOCTURNE was founded on a singular conviction: that dinner is theatre, and every table deserves the best seat.

Our room is a study in living contrasts — low light against bright technique, smoke against citrus, crisp linen against reclaimed walnut. It is an atmosphere built with the same precision as our plates.

Our Philosophy

Three pillars of the night

01

Fire-Led Craft

Oak and charcoal feed our live-fire pass. Every temperature is recorded to the degree, every ember turned by hand — an ancient technique treated with modern precision.

02

Rare & Considered

We source with restraint — a single truffle purveyor, small farms, and a cellar of 1,200 labels curated by hand. Scarcity here is a virtue, never a stunt.

03

Midnight Hospitality

Our maître d', chefs and sommeliers anticipate before you ask. Hospitality here is intuition — warm, unhurried, and beautifully attuned to the hour.
