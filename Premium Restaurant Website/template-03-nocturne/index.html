Our Story

A table set for the senses

Born in a landmark Chicago address, NOCTURNE celebrates the theatre of dinner — low light, high technique, and plates that arrive like scenes. Each course is composed like a movement: texture, aroma and colour in deliberate harmony.

Our kitchen works with small farms and a single truffle purveyor, letting each season write its own chapter on the menu. The room is a study in midnight glamour — velvet, brass and candlelight.
