Our Story

Built on wood & fire

We traded gas flames for oak and charcoal the day we opened. Since then, every steak, prawn and vegetable that leaves our pass has been kissed by real fire — never a grill pan in sight.

Our kitchen is loud, our cellar is bold, and our philosophy is simple: season hard, burn clean, serve fast.
