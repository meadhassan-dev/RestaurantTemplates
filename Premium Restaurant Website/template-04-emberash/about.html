Our Roots

Born in smoke

We started as a four-table grill room above a barbershop. No gas line, no fryer — just oak, charcoal and a bet that people would drive across town for a properly fire-cooked steak.

Eight years later the bet paid off. The grill room is a 60-seat fire kitchen, the oak comes from a family farm in Missouri, and every plate still passes over real coals — the way it did on day one.

The Grill Room
What We Stand For

Three rules of fire

01

Burn Real Wood

No gas jets, no shortcuts. Oak, hickory and charcoal give our food its soul — and our room its smell.

02

Low & Slow

Our ribs smoke for twelve hours, our sauces reduce overnight. Fire demands patience, and we give it willingly.

03

Keep It Loud

We're a kitchen that laughs, a room that cheers and a restaurant that never whispers. Bold food, bold energy.
